# Nerdcraft's Vanilla+ Additions

a vanilla-style building and quality-of-life mod for the Nerdcraft SMP, built for Minecraft 1.26 (Fabric).

## Description

Nerdcraft's Vanilla+ Additions fills the gaps vanilla leaves behind for builders. It adds brick variants or granite, diorite and andesite (stairs, slabs and walls included), introduces a new smooth polished blocks and missing vanilla building blocks like polished stone walls.

On top of the new blocks, the mod bundles a bunch of practical recipe and gameplay tweaks: coral blocks and amethyst growths become craftable, bee nests can be made by hand, potions finally stack, and you can accelerate copper weathering with a splash of water.

Everything is implemented to feel like genuine vanilla content — same block properties, same tags, same recipes.

## Features

### New Blocks

**Brick variants** (all with stairs, slabs and walls):

- Andesite Bricks
- Diorite Bricks
- Granite Bricks

**Vanilla improvements**:

- Polished Andesite Wall
- Polished Diorite Wall
- Polished Granite Wall

**Smooth Polished Block Variants:**

- Smooth Granite
- Smooth Andesite
- Smooth Diorite
- Smooth Tuff
- Smooth Blackstone
- Smooth Deepslate

### Recipes

- **Coral blocks** — craft any coral block from 4 of its coral plants *or* 4 of its coral fans (both living and dead variants).
- **Amethyst buds & clusters** — craft from amethyst shards: small bud (1), medium bud (2), large bud (3), cluster (4).
- **Bee nest** — craftable from 4 wheat + 4 honeycomb.
- **Smelting conversions** — smelt andesite into tuff and diorite into calcite.
- **Stonecutter recipes** — every block family converts freely between its raw, polished, smooth polished and brick forms on the stonecutter (plus stairs/slabs/walls).

### Gameplay Changes

- **Potion stacking** — regular, splash and lingering potions now stack up to 16.
- **Copper weathering** — right-click any copper block with a water bottle to advance it one weathering stage (consumes the bottle and returns a glass bottle, with scrape particles). Applies to all copper variants, including cut copper, doors, trapdoors, bars, chains, bulbs, grates, lanterns, chests and the copper golem.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.26
- Fabric Loader 0.19.3+
- Fabric API
